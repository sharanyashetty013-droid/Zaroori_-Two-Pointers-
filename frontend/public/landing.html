How it works

1Pick a language
Aअಕ

The ASHA didi sits with her and chooses Hindi, Kannada or English.

2Six questions, read aloud

Big buttons, one question per screen. No reading needed.

3A second-opinion plan

Questions to ask the doctor and a nearby government hospital.

4An anonymous slip

A PDF she can carry to the clinic. No name on it.

5If she is being forced

The screen turns red and shows her rights and a helpline. With her consent, an NGO or officer is alerted the same day.

6Patterns, not people

Health officials see district counts and flagged hospitals, never a woman’s identity.
